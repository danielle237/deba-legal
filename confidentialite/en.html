PRIVACY POLICY — DEBA CI

Last updated: 11 octobre 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how W2D CONSULTING, publisher of the DEBA CI application ("we", "us"), collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data. As DEBA CI is intended to be available in several African countries, this policy relies on a common regional foundation, supplemented by the national law applicable in your country of residence:

  • the African Union Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection (the "Malabo Convention", 2014), which sets out the common data-protection principles applicable across the continent;
  • ECOWAS Supplementary Act A/SA.1/01/10 on Personal Data Protection, applicable within the Economic Community of West African States;
  • the national personal-data-protection law of your country of residence where one exists — for example, in Côte d'Ivoire, Law No. 2013-450 of June 19, 2013, overseen by the Telecommunications/ICT Regulatory Authority of Côte d'Ivoire (ARTCI); in countries where the Application is available and which have an equivalent authority or law, that law applies in the same way;
  • in the absence of a specific national law in your country of residence, DEBA CI applies, as a minimum standard, the principles of the Malabo Convention and the ECOWAS Supplementary Act referred to above.

2. Data We Collect

2.1. Data provided directly by you

  • Phone number (account identifier, verified by OTP);
  • Profile information: first name, last name, photo, city/country, and depending on the modules used: professional information (craftsperson, chef, coach, agency), shop information (My Business), CVs/applications (Career), school documents (Schools), etc.;
  • Content that you publish: listings, reviews, messages, photos, videos, documents (e.g., lost/found documents);
  • Responses to polls and satisfaction surveys.

2.2. Data collected automatically

  • Location data (approximate or precise position), used only when you activate "near me" functions or for modules that require it (delivery, facility booking, etc.) — you may disable location services in your phone's settings at any time, with the possible limitation of certain features;
  • Technical usage data (usage statistics, pages viewed, device type) via Firebase Analytics;
  • Push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging), used to send you notifications relating to your activity (messages, order status, reminders);
  • Technical logs (server logs) for security purposes and incident resolution.

2.3. Payment-related data Payments are processed by our provider GeniusPay. We receive only the confirmation and reference of the transaction, never your full banking or mobile money credentials, which are managed exclusively by GeniusPay in accordance with its own privacy policy.

2.4. Data related to application permissions Depending on the features used, the Application may request access to: the camera (taking photos for listings/profiles/documents), storage/gallery (importing photos), the microphone (voice messages, calls if enabled), location, Bluetooth (connecting to a receipt printer for merchants), phone calls (directly contacting an advertiser), notifications. Each permission is requested at the time of its actual use and may be revoked at any time in the phone's settings.

3. Purposes of Processing

We use your data to:

  • create and manage your account, and authenticate you;
  • provide, personalize and improve the Application's features;
  • facilitate connections between Users (listings, reservations, messaging);
  • process payments through our provider;
  • send you notifications relating to your activity;
  • ensure the security of the Application, prevent fraud and abuse, and handle moderation reports;
  • comply with our legal and regulatory obligations;
  • produce anonymized or aggregated usage statistics.

4. Legal Basis

Depending on the case, processing is based on: performance of the terms of use (Terms) that you have accepted, your consent (e.g., location, notifications), our legitimate interest (security, improvement of the Service, statistics), or compliance with a legal obligation.

5. Data Sharing

We never sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • GeniusPay, for payment processing;
  • our SMS/WhatsApp delivery provider, for OTP verification;
  • Firebase / Google, for usage analysis and push notifications (data may then be processed on servers located outside Côte d'Ivoire, subject to appropriate security safeguards);
  • the competent authorities, where required by law or to protect our rights, our safety or that of our Users;
  • other Users, for information that you choose to make public (listings, professional profile, reviews).

6. Retention Period

Your data is retained for the duration of your active account, then archived or deleted within a reasonable period after account deletion, unless a longer legal retention obligation applies (e.g., transaction data for accounting/tax purposes) or it is necessary to handle an ongoing dispute.

7. Your Rights

In accordance with the instruments referred to in Article 1 (the Malabo Convention, the ECOWAS Supplementary Act, and the national law of your country of residence where one exists), you have the right to access, rectify, object to and delete your personal data, as well as the right to withdraw your consent where processing depends on it. You may exercise these rights directly from the Application's settings (profile modification, account deletion) or by contacting us at danywanda@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the personal-data-protection authority competent in your country of residence — for example, in Côte d'Ivoire, the Telecommunications/ICT Regulatory Authority of Côte d'Ivoire (ARTCI).

8. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data (encryption of communications, token-based authentication, separation of administrator access, security logging). As no system is entirely infallible, we encourage you to never share your verification codes (OTP) with a third party.

9. Minors

The Application is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data concerning children under 13 without the consent of a legal representative. If you believe that a child has provided us with data without authorization, please contact us so that we may delete it.

10. Amendment of This Policy

We may amend this Privacy Policy. Any substantial amendment will be notified to you via the Application before it takes effect.

11. Contact

For any question relating to your personal data: danywanda@gmail.com — W2D CONSULTING, Ton adresse, Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire.
