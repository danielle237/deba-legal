Delete my DEBA CI account

Last updated: 11 octobre 2026

This page explains how to request the deletion of your DEBA CI account and the associated data.

From the app (the easiest way)

  • Open the DEBA CI app and sign in.
  • Go to Settings, then Delete my account.
  • If you wish, first download a copy of your personal data (JSON file).
  • Confirm your request (with your PIN code if you created one).

30-day grace period

Your account is not erased immediately. For 30 days it stays usable and you can cancel the deletion at any time: Settings, Delete my account, then Cancel deletion. We send a reminder a few days before the end of the period. After that, deletion is automatic and permanent.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: first and last name, nickname, photo, date of birth, phone number, password and PIN code.
  • Your sign-ins and devices, notifications, favourites and preferences.
  • Your interests and activity (predictions, quizzes, workouts, reminders, baskets…).
  • Your listings and professional profiles (ads, job offers, craftsperson, chef, coach or artist profiles…).
  • Your messages (the content is erased), your applications and CVs, your verification identity documents.
  • Your place in the organisations and teams you belonged to (the displayed name becomes "Former member").

What is kept, without your identity

Some information must be kept to meet accounting, tax or security obligations, or because it also belongs to other people. It is anonymised: your name, phone number and address are removed.

  • Payment transactions, withdrawals and receipts (amounts, dates, references).
  • Entries of a team's or association's cash book (the member's name becomes "Former member").
  • Orders and bookings already paid, and moderation history.
  • Reviews and content visible to others, without the author's name.

Before requesting deletion

  • Withdraw your earnings balance: an account with money left to withdraw cannot be deleted.
  • If you are the manager of an organisation that has other members, transfer the responsibility first.
  • Payments in progress (protected purchase, escrow) must be completed.

No longer have access to the app?

Write to us at danywanda@gmail.com from the address linked to your request, stating the phone number of the account to delete. We will verify your identity before acting.
